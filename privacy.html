Privacy Policy

App: Captain’s License Test Prep (iPhone, iPad and Android)
Developer: MD Shahariar Nazim Joy
Contact: dmjks120@gmail.com
Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary: This app is private by design. We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account, no login and no sign-up, all of your study data stays on your device, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, contacts, location, or health data. It does not ask for your name, email, or any account credentials. Because there is no server that receives your information, your personal data is never transmitted to us. The app does not use advertising identifiers.

2. Study data stored on your device

Your progress, mastery scores, bookmarks, mistake bank, flashcard schedule, mock-exam history, streaks, focus sessions, and settings are stored locally on your device in the app’s private storage. You can permanently erase this data at any time using Settings → Reset All Progress inside the app, or by deleting the app.

3. Optional iCloud sync (iPhone and iPad)

On Apple devices with iCloud enabled, your study data may sync across your own Apple devices through Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) using your personal iCloud account. This data is stored in your iCloud and is not accessible to the developer. The Android app does not sync data anywhere.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play on Android. The store handles your payment and manages your subscription; the app receives only the purchase status needed to unlock features. We never see or store your payment details, Apple ID, or Google account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Read-aloud

The optional “read question aloud” feature uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The app sends no speech data to any server.

6. Store privacy labels

Consistent with the above, the app is declared as “Data Not Collected” on its App Store privacy label and as collecting and sharing no data in its Google Play Data safety section, and it is not used to track you.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional mariner credential and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete on our side. You remain in full control of your on-device data and can delete it instantly as described above. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised policy on this page.
