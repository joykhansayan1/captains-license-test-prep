How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the app and go to Settings → Restore Purchases (a Restore Purchases button also appears on the unlock screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID (iPhone/iPad) or Google account (Android) used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, select Captain’s License Test Prep, and choose Cancel. On Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select the app, and choose Cancel. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — the yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. You won’t be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

What’s included?

A full unlock gives you 2,000+ practice questions across Rules of the Road, Navigation General, Deck General & Safety, and Chart Plotting; 20 quick-reference sheets; flashcards with spaced repetition; timed mock exams at official pass marks; a mistake bank; study guides; and progress tracking.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All questions and study tools are stored on your device, so you can study anywhere — no internet required.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Reset All Progress in the app, or delete the app. All study data is stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
